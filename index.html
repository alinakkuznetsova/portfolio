New Natures

A comparative study of three ways to combine neural cellular automata trained on natural textures, testing whether linear mode connectivity, a property studied on image classifiers, holds for models that grow their output.

The combinations that held became a series of pattern designs, and the best of them run live in an interactive exhibition in the browser.

The Rematch

A real chess board that plays back. An overhead camera reads my moves, Stockfish answers at 2750 Elo, Deep Blue's estimated strength in 1997, and its pieces appear on the board as projected light.

I performed it live as a restaging of Deep Blue against Kasparov. The system ran from first move to checkmate in front of an audience without interruption.

Borderless

Visitors sketch a mushroom, a planet or a cloud. A fine-tuned diffusion model repaints it in watercolour and places it in a shared 360° world that you can walk into in VR.

I owned the machine learning: a cleaned dataset of around 600 images, four LoRA fine-tunes on Stable Diffusion 1.5, each fixing a named failure in the last, ControlNet sketch conditioning tuned per category, and the backend that turns a browser drawing into a placed asset.

Piblo the Plutonian

A Plutonian who makes a 10,000-record space-mission dataset worth talking to. Piblo remembers what you tell it, writes you a story with a language model small enough to run on a laptop without a GPU, and answers questions about real missions in plain English.

Twenty people tested it. Almost all used it without help and called it fun; fewer noticed it was teaching them, which set the brief for the next version.

Colophon

For me, visual design and engineering aren't separate skills but a balance, and the work is best when both are held to the same standard. This site is an attempt to show that. The figures are drawn live in your browser from ten layers of ASCII, split by brightness and shifting as you move. Set in Newsreader and IBM Plex Mono, with International Klein Blue as the only accent, and built in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
